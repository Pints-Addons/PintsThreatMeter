Pint's Threat Meter - Changelog

v1.2.4
- Everyone in your group gets a bar on the mob you're watching, at 0 until they've done something, so a tank sees
  the others coming instead of only once they've built threat (pets show once they have some).
- WoW Forever: when the game hides someone's threat in a fight they still get a bar - the game draws it and writes
  their percent (100% = pulls aggro); they sit below the ones that can be read and sorted.

v1.2.3
- WoW Forever: hover a player's bar in the game's Damage Meter for what it came from, spell by spell (amount and
  share). Hold Shift for it by mob. Works for whatever the meter window shows (damage, healing...). The game hides the
  amounts during a fight, so the breakdown shows once it ends. /pth metertips turns it off or on.

v1.2.2
- WoW Forever: fixed a flood of errors in combat when the game hides who a mob or player is.

v1.2.1
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.

v1.2
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Threat or buffs the game keeps hidden are skipped instead of causing errors.
- Tank detection remembers your last known state when the game hides it in combat.

v1.1
- First public release as Pint's Threat Meter.
- Group threat on your target with you always shown, pull bar, time to pull, threat per second,
  scaled or raw percent, healer mode, tank mode, warnings with sound and flash.
