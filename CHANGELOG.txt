Pint's Threat Meter - Changelog

v1.2
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Threat or buffs the game keeps hidden are skipped instead of causing errors.
- Tank detection remembers your last known state when the game hides it in combat.

v1.1
- First public release as Pint's Threat Meter.
- Group threat on your target with you always shown, pull bar, time to pull, threat per second,
  scaled or raw percent, healer mode, tank mode, warnings with sound and flash.
