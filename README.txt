Pint's Threat Meter v1.1 - your group's threat on your target, at a glance
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsThreatMeter
  Restart the game.

THE METER
  One bar for everyone in your group on your target's threat list (10 bars to start), with YOU always
  shown even when you're further down. The header shows the target's name.
  - Threat amount, percent and threat per second on each bar (each can be turned off).
  - Scaled percent: 100% means you'd pull aggro. Or raw percent of the tank's threat
    (pulling at 110% in melee and 130% at range).
  - Pull bar: a red bar showing how much threat it takes to pull aggro from the tank.
  - Time to pull: at your current threat per second, how many seconds until you pull.
  - Class colours, or your own colours for you, the tank and everyone else.
  - Players out of melee range can look the same, faded or darkened.
  - Healer mode: target a friend to see the threat on whatever they're attacking.
  - Hide it never, out of combat, when solo, or both.

WARNINGS
  A warning with sound and a screen flash when your threat passes a set percent (85% to start),
  with a cooldown between warnings and a minimum threat to ignore trash.
  Tank mode (automatic in Defensive Stance, Bear Form and Righteous Fury, or Always / Never):
  you're warned when someone else gets close to pulling off you instead.

OPTIONS  (/pth, or right-click the minimap button)
  Bars, width, bar height, text size, scale, background, colours, what each bar shows,
  warnings (percent, sound, flash, cooldown), tank mode, lock position.
  Drag the meter to move it. Already using ThreatClassic2? /pth off-tc2 switches it off.

COMMANDS
  /pth            options (also /threatmeter)
  /pth toggle     show or hide the meter
  /pth test       test the warning
  /pth minimap    show or hide the minimap button
